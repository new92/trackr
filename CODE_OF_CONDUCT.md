# Code of Conduct

We aim to maintain a welcoming and constructive open-source community.

Be respectful, avoid harassment and discriminatory or abusive language, stay
on topic, and give actionable technical feedback. Respect privacy: do not
publish anyone's location data, credentials, or personal details without consent.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or block participants who repeatedly
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer
at the contact address in `SECURITY.md`.
